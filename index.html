🔥 Smoke & air quality — check each morning
Interior BC and the Rockies are smoke-prone in August. Save the biggest hikes (Iceline, Plain of Six Glaciers) for the clearest-air mornings, and ease off when the AQHI hits 7 or higher — the official "avoid strenuous outdoor activity" line. N95s in the car; recirculate the A/C on smoky drives.
